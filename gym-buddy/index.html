BEGINNER RULES

Train smart

  • Use a weight you can control for every rep.
  • Finish most sets with about 2–3 clean reps still possible.
  • For single-arm work, start with your left arm and match its reps on the right.
  • Do not chase soreness or failure. Chase clean, repeatable technique.
  • Belly and love-handle fat reduces through overall fat loss—not spot reduction.
